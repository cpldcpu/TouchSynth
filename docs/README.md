## Figures (`figures/`)

`s9_osc` (DCO vs DAC spectrograms, model), `s9_hw` (board 1 three-oscillator takes vs model), `s9_hwcmp`
(DCO vs DAC on board 1), `s10_5v` (5 V core: cutoff and THD), `s12_mixer` (mixer: response to the line out and the ADC, ground rejection), `s12_mixer_2bus` (two-bus mixer:
response, ground rejection of the mirror variants, clipping with 8 hard-panned cards), `s13_pan` (pan stage).
`voice_pcb/`: the voice card PCB (layers, silkscreen, placement, 3D renders `render_*.png`).
`backplane_pcb/`: the backplane PCB, the same set (`hw/pcb_figures.py`; not-fitted parts are left out of the 3D
renders).

## Audio (`audio/`)

- `s9_osc/`: model renders of the oscillator variants (`a_dco` … `h_dac_188k_rc`), the three-oscillator voice
  (`moog_*`), board 1 takes against the model (`hw_moog_*`, `model_moog_*`), DCO against DAC on board 1
  (`cmp_*`), the kettle on the 187.5 kHz carrier (`hw_kettle_188k`), full against half rate on board 1
  (`hw_full_rate_*`, `hw_half_rate_*`; the `moog_open` pair and the scale's top notes from a faulty experiment build).
- `suite_moog_hw.mp3`: the TouchSynth suite recorded on board 1 with the Toccata's top voice on the
  three-oscillator DAC voice.
